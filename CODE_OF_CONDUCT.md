# Code Of Conduct

SYNCIO welcomes respectful technical collaboration.

Participants must:

- discuss ideas and implementations without personal attacks;
- respect privacy and never repost another person's credentials or account data;
- give actionable feedback and assume good faith;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and unwanted personal contact.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
